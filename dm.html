What is CPUD?

CPUD(Chemical Proper Usage and Dispose) is a project led by LabPath, MSF Korea, Mappers Unbound

The project aims for encouraging people to avoid drug abuse by understanding drug's effect on our body, as well as notifying where they can dispose the medicines.
