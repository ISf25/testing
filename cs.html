Chemical Stewardship


We need to use chemicals and dispose it properly to minimize the environmental harm


What is CPUD?

CPUD(Chemical Proper Usage and Dispose) is a project led by LabPath, Mappers Unbound, MSF Korea.

The project aims for encouraging people to avoid drug abuse by understanding drug's effect on our body, as well as notifying where they can dispose the medicines.
